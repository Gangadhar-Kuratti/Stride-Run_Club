ABOUT STRIDE

A community built around running consistently

OUR MISSION

Stride is a community built around running consistently, meeting new people and enjoying the process. We believe that running is better together and that everyone deserves a supportive community to help them stay active.

OUR VALUES

🏃

RUN

Consistent movement for body and mind. We believe in the power of regular running to improve physical and mental health.

🤝

CONNECT

Meet people who share your passion. Running together creates friendships and support networks that extend beyond the run.

📈

GROW

Improve together, whatever your pace. We celebrate personal progress and support each other's goals.

WHO CAN JOIN

Everyone is welcome at Stride Run Club. Whether you're:

  • Complete beginner who has never run before
  • Returning to running after a break
  • Experienced runner looking for company
  • Training for a specific race or distance
  • Just looking to get moving and meet people

There's a place for you here. We have runs for different paces and distances, and we always ensure no one gets left behind.

WHAT HAPPENS DURING A RUN

10 minutes before

Arrival & Check-in

Arrive at the starting point, say hello to fellow runners, and get ready.

Start time

Welcome & Briefing

Quick welcome, introduce new faces, and go over the route for the day.

During the run

The Run

We run together at a conversational pace. Groups form naturally based on pace, and we regroup at points along the route.

After the run

Cool Down & Social

Stretch together and optional coffee/chat for those who want to stay and socialize.

BEGINNER-FRIENDLY

No Experience Needed

You don't need any running experience to join. Many of our members started as complete beginners.

Walk-Run Welcome

Walking and running combinations are totally fine. Listen to your body and do what feels right.

Supportive Environment

Our community is supportive and encouraging. Everyone starts somewhere, and we celebrate every step.

Short Distances Available

We offer shorter distance options for beginners. You can always build up gradually.

CLUB ETIQUETTE

Be On Time

Please arrive on time so we can start together as a group.

Stay Together

We run as a group. If you're faster, slow down occasionally. If you're slower, we'll wait for you.

Be Friendly

Say hello to fellow runners, introduce yourself to new faces, and be inclusive.

Listen to Your Body

It's okay to walk, take breaks, or cut a run short. Your health comes first.

Respect the Route

Follow the planned route, be aware of your surroundings, and share paths with others.

READY TO JOIN US?

Sign up for your first run and experience the Stride community.
